How Much Mulch Do I Need?

Estimate how much mulch you need for garden beds, landscaping, tree rings, borders, and planting areas. Enter the area and planned depth to calculate cubic yards and plan your material order.

Mulch is commonly sold by the cubic yard in bulk or by bag volume. Your final order can vary with the material, settling, bed shape, and the depth you choose, so use the result as a planning estimate.
