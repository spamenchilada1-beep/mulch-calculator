Why calculate cubic yards first?

Bulk mulch is often discussed in cubic yards, while bagged mulch is sold in individual bags. Calculating the project volume first gives you a consistent starting point before you compare products.

How do I convert cubic yards to bags?

Use the coverage or volume stated on the exact bag. Product packaging can vary, so do not assume that every bag covers the same area or contains the same amount of material.

Bag quantity = recommended project volume ÷ bag volume

When the package gives square-foot coverage at a specified depth instead of cubic volume, use that stated coverage to determine how many bags cover your recommended area.

Example planning method

For a rectangular bed, calculate the cubic-yard requirement with length × width × depth ÷ 324. Then apply the product's stated bag coverage or volume to convert the recommended quantity into whole bags.
