How to calculate mulch in cubic yards

For a rectangular area, multiply length by width to get square feet. Then multiply the area by the planned depth in feet to get cubic feet, and divide by 27 to convert cubic feet to cubic yards.

Cubic yards = length × width × depth in feet ÷ 27

When your depth is entered in inches, divide the inch measurement by 12 before using the formula.

Example: a 20 × 10 ft bed at 3 inches

A 20 × 10 ft bed has 200 square feet. At 3 inches deep, the depth is 0.25 feet, so the project needs about 1.85 cubic yards before any planning allowance. With ATHENA's 10% planning allowance, the planning quantity is about 2.04 cubic yards.

How to handle irregular beds

Break an irregular landscape bed into smaller rectangles or other simple sections. Calculate each section separately, then add the cubic-yard results together before planning the order.

How much mulch should I order?

Use the calculated volume as the starting point, then review the product depth and coverage requirements for the exact mulch you are buying. Coverage can vary by material, depth, compaction, and how the product is delivered.

Bulk mulch vs. bags

Bulk mulch is commonly quoted by cubic yard. Bagged mulch is usually sold by the individual bag, so a cubic-yard estimate must be converted using the bag's stated coverage or volume. See How Many Bags of Mulch Do I Need? for the bag-planning method.
