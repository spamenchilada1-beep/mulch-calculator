Enter the length and width of the area in feet and the planned mulch depth in inches in the ATHENA Mulch Calculator. The calculator reports cubic yards and a recommended order with a 10% planning allowance.

How to calculate mulch

For a rectangular area, multiply length by width by depth in inches, then divide by 324 to convert the result to cubic yards.

Cubic yards = length × width × depth in inches ÷ 324

How much extra mulch should I order?

ATHENA adds 10% to the calculated volume as a planning allowance. This can help account for normal measuring variation and material needs during installation.

How much mulch do I need for multiple beds?

For irregular or multiple beds, divide the project into simple sections, calculate each section separately, and add the cubic-yard results. Keep the planned depth consistent unless different areas require different coverage.

What about bags of mulch?

This calculator reports cubic yards. To estimate bag quantities, use the coverage printed on the specific bag or product package because bag volume and stated coverage vary.
