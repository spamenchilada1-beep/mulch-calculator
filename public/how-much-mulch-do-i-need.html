How Much Mulch Do I Need?

Estimate the cubic yards of mulch needed for garden beds, planting areas, and landscaping projects from length, width, and depth.

How to calculate mulch

For a rectangular area, multiply length by width by depth in inches, then divide by 324 to convert the result to cubic yards.

Cubic yards = length × width × depth in inches ÷ 324

Example: a 20 × 10 ft bed at 3 inches

A 20 × 10 ft bed has 200 square feet. At 3 inches deep, the base quantity is about 1.85 cubic yards. ATHENA's 10% planning allowance brings the planning quantity to about 2.04 cubic yards.

How much extra mulch should I order?

ATHENA adds 10% to the calculated volume as a planning allowance. This can help account for normal measuring variation and material needs during installation, but the final quantity should reflect your product and project conditions.

How much mulch do I need for multiple beds?

For irregular or multiple beds, divide the project into simple sections, calculate each section separately, and add the cubic-yard results. Keep the planned depth consistent unless different areas require different coverage.

How do I convert cubic yards to bags?

Bagged mulch is sold in different package sizes, so use the volume stated on the exact bag. A simple planning formula is:

Bags needed = cubic yards × 27 ÷ cubic feet per bag

Apply your planning allowance before converting to bags, then round up to a whole bag.

What should I check before ordering?

Confirm the product type, stated bag volume or bulk coverage, planned depth, delivery quantity, and the supplier's ordering increments. For bulk mulch, compare the cubic-yard result directly with the supplier's quoted quantity.
